# LooseCode Community Rules & Ethics Rulebook

**Version:** 2.6  
**Last Updated:** 2026  
**Enforcement Scope:** Discord Server, WhatsApp Communities, Hackathons, Live Sprints, Open-Source Repositories, and Official LooseCode Events.

---

## 1. Hackathon & Sprint Integrity

### 1.1 Fresh Code Principle
Projects submitted for evaluation in LooseCode hackathons and sprints must be conceived and coded within the designated event timeframe.
- Open-source libraries, frameworks, UI kits, templates, and public APIs are welcomed, provided they are explicitly documented and credited in the project `README.md`.
- Submitting pre-existing proprietary software or passing off turn-key templates as original engineering results in immediate disqualification.

### 1.2 Generative AI & Assisted Coding Policy
Modern builders use modern tools. LLMs, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and automated assistants are fully permitted as multipliers.
- The fundamental architecture, problem-solving, custom logic, and creative cohesion must stem from the builder team.
- Team members must be capable of defending, explaining, and live-debugging any system in their submission during judges' teardowns.

### 1.3 Proof of Deployment & Open Source Verification
- All submissions must provide a public repository with transparent commit histories.
- A functional live deployment URL or demonstrable local walkthrough video is required.
- "Proof of work" is our sole evaluation metric.

---

## 2. Code of Conduct & Interpersonal Ethics

### 2.1 Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Discrimination
LooseCode is committed to providing an inclusive, harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, nationality, age, or background.
- Offensive speech, unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, stalking, and continuous disruption of events will result in immediate removal.

### 2.2 Constructive Technical Critique
- Critique the code, not the creator.
- Dissect algorithms, debate architectural patterns, and push for optimization—always with professional respect.
- Elitist gatekeeping, condescension, and dismissing beginner questions are strictly prohibited.

### 2.3 Fair Attribution & Team Credit
- Credit all contributors honestly: developers, designers, prompt engineers, researchers, and product leads.
- Never strip or obscure a teammate's name from git histories, submission portals, or pitch decks.

---

## 3. Intellectual Property & Ownership

### 3.1 100% Builder Ownership
- **You own 100% of everything you build at LooseCode.**
- LooseCode claims zero equity, zero IP rights, and zero royalties over projects created during our hackathons or challenges.
- LooseCode is a launchpad and arena, not an incubator taxing your creation.

### 3.2 Open Source Responsibilities
- Public repositories should include an explicit open-source license (MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL, etc.).
- Comply with all third-party license requirements and terms of service. Never leak proprietary company secrets or credentials into public repositories.

---

## 4. Community Channels & Safety (Discord & WhatsApp)

### 4.1 No Spam or Unsolicited Promotion
- Mass direct messaging, unsolicited commercial pitches, multi-level marketing, and cryptocurrency pump-and-dump promotion are banned.
- Use designated channels (`#showcase`, `#find-a-team`, `#tech-support`) for relevant discussions.

### 4.2 Security & Data Privacy
- Never share API secret keys, environment tokens, private credentials, or personally identifiable information (PII).
- Malicious exploits, phishing links, and malware uploads lead to an instant global ban.

---

## 5. Reporting & Enforcement Protocol

Infractions are handled via a three-tier progressive enforcement protocol:
1. **Level 1 (Notice):** Formal warning and deletion of offending content.
2. **Level 2 (Suspension):** Temporary ban from channels and current hackathon participation.
3. **Level 3 (Expulsion):** Permanent global exclusion from LooseCode events, Discord, WhatsApp groups, and partner tracks.

**Incident Reporting:**
- Contact any Core Builder / Moderator on Discord.
- Email: `loosecodeofficial@gmail.com`
- All inquiries are handled with strict confidentiality.
